# Support Mini Diarium

Mini Diarium is MIT-licensed and free to use. No ads, no telemetry, no account, and no
features held back behind a paywall: the desktop app you download is the whole app.

If it is useful to you and you want to chip in, here are the ways. All of them are
optional, and nothing in the app changes either way. Starring the repo, reporting bugs,
translating, and telling people about it help just as much.

### Bitcoin over Lightning

Lightning address. Paste it into any wallet that supports Lightning addresses or LNURL-pay
(Cake Wallet, Phoenix, Zeus, Blink, Wallet of Satoshi, and others):

```text

```

Lightning is the best option for small amounts. Fees are negligible and it settles in
seconds instead of waiting for on-chain confirmations.

## A few practical notes

- **Match the network.** Send BTC only on Bitcoin mainnet to the `bc1...` address, and XMR
  only on Monero mainnet to the `4...` address. Nobody can recover crypto sent on the
  wrong chain.
- **Copy addresses from this file on `master`**, at
  [github.com/fjrevoredo/mini-diarium](https://github.com/fjrevoredo/mini-diarium/blob/master/DONATE.md),
  rather than from a screenshot, a chat message, an issue comment, or a fork. Swapped
  donation addresses are a known trick in open source, and this file is the canonical copy.
  If it ever disagrees with the README, please open an issue before sending anything.
- **Donations are gifts, not purchases.** No invoices or refunds, and no obligation in
  either direction. Feature requests are free and welcome whether or not you donate, and I
  weigh them against [PHILOSOPHY.md](PHILOSOPHY.md) on merit.
- Bitcoin transactions are public and traceable. If you would rather the donation not be
  linkable to you, Monero or Lightning is the better pick.
