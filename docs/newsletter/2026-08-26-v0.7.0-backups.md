**Subject:** Mini Diarium 0.7.0: backups you can finally see

---

You've had automatic backups since day one. You just couldn't see them.

That changes in 0.7.0.

We have taken a lot of care in this new big update in making backups a first-class citizen of the app.

You have now a full "backup" section in the preferences of the app where you can see the status of the backups, its location and everything you need to manage them.

Open Preferences, and every snapshot is right there: when it was taken, how many entries it holds, whether it's been checked. The same view works from the lock screen too, before you type a password, since that's exactly when you need it most.

Restore the whole journal to a point in time, or pull back just the entries you're missing. Either way, nothing you already have gets touched.

We also closed the other way an entry could disappear: erasing one and clicking away used to delete it silently. Now it asks first.

Plus: design polish, richer notifications, and a quiet way to support the project if you want to.

As always, feel free to reach me through the github project or the project's email if you have any questions, suggestions or comments.
